The Voice

Hockey parenting is complicated. The pressure, the politics, the blind spots — most parents are too close to see it clearly. John Paris Jr. has spent decades inside this game, and he is not here to comfort you. He is here to educate you.

Parental Love Syndrome is the direct, honest conversation hockey families have been avoiding. Grounded in psychology. Built on real experience. No bias. No sugarcoating.

"You love your kid. That's exactly the problem."

— The thesis of Parental Love Syndrome
